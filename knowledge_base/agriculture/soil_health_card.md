# Soil Health Card Scheme & Fertilizer Guidelines
## Metadata
- **Category**: Agriculture & Crop Advice
- **Source**: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, National Project on Soil Health
- **Objective**: Provide farmers with soil nutrient status (12 parameters: N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, Bo, pH, EC, OC) and customized fertilizer recommendations to optimize yield and reduce unnecessary input costs.
- **Cycle**: Soil sampling carried out once every 3 years for all agricultural holdings.
- **Key Fertilizer Best Practices**:
  1. **Balanced NPK**: Do not rely solely on Urea. Overuse of nitrogen without adequate Phosphorous and Potash leads to soil acidity and crop lodging.
  2. **Secondary & Micronutrients**: Sulphur and Zinc are critically deficient in many Indo-Gangetic and black soils. Apply Zinc Sulphate (25 kg/ha) at basal stage where recommended.
  3. **Organic Carbon**: Incorporate farmyard manure (FYM) or green manuring (Dhaincha/Sunn hemp) to maintain organic carbon above 0.5%.
  4. **Bio-fertilizers**: Use Rhizobium seed treatment for pulses, Azotobacter / PSB for cereals.
- **Access Portal**: soilhealth.dac.gov.in
