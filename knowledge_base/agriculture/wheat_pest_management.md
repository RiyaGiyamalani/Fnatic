# Wheat Crop Disease Management: Yellow Rust and Aphids
## Metadata
- **Category**: Agriculture & Crop Advice
- **Source**: Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) & Directorate of Wheat Development
- **Target Crop**: Wheat (Triticum aestivum), Rabi Season
- **Disease: Yellow Rust (Stripe Rust - Puccinia striiformis)**:
  - **Symptoms**: Yellow-colored pustules arranged in linear stripes on the leaf blades. Yellow powder easily rubs off onto fingers or white cloth. Commonly occurs during cool and humid weather.
  - **Non-Chemical & Cultural Prevention**:
    - Grow resistant wheat cultivars recommended for the zone (e.g., HD 3086, DBW 187, DBW 222, PBW 725).
    - Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilization; balanced NPK application (120:60:40 kg/ha).
    - Maintain proper field drainage to prevent localized humidity.
  - **Recommended Management**:
    - Monitor fields weekly during January and February.
    - At first appearance of pustules, spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 0.1% (1 ml per liter of water / 200 ml in 200 liters of water per acre) or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1 ml/L. Repeat after 15 days if cloudy weather persists.
- **Pest: Wheat Aphids**:
  - **Management**: If aphid population exceeds 10-15 aphids per earhead, apply neem oil spray (3000 ppm @ 3 ml/L) or Dimethoate 30% EC (1.5 ml/L).
- **Advisory Advisory Note**: Always consult the local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or Block Agriculture Officer before chemical application.
