# Maternal and Child Health: Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY) & ASHA Guidance
## Metadata
- **Category**: General Health Information
- **Source**: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), National Health Mission (NHM)
- **Objective**: Reduce maternal and neonatal mortality by promoting institutional delivery among poor pregnant women.
- **Cash Assistance for Institutional Delivery**:
  - In Low Performing States (LPS: UP, Bihar, MP, Rajasthan, Odisha, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Uttarakhand, Assam, J&K):
    - Rural mother receives Rs. 1,400.
    - ASHA incentive for escorting and tracking: Rs. 600.
  - In High Performing States (HPS):
    - Rural BPL mother receives Rs. 700.
    - ASHA incentive: Rs. 600.
- **Antenatal Care (ANC) Guidelines**:
  - Mandatory minimum 4 ANC checkups:
    1. 1st checkup within 12 weeks of pregnancy (early registration).
    2. 2nd checkup between 14-26 weeks.
    3. 3rd checkup between 28-34 weeks.
    4. 4th checkup between 36 weeks and delivery.
  - Supplementation: Daily 1 Iron-Folic Acid (IFA) tablet for minimum 180 days after first trimester, plus Calcium tablets. Two doses of Tetanus Toxoid (TT / Td) injection.
- **Emergency Transport Service**: Call 102 (National Ambulance Service for pregnant women and sick infants) or 108 (Emergency response).
