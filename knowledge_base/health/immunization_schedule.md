# Universal Immunization Programme (UIP) India Schedule
## Metadata
- **Category**: General Health Information
- **Source**: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) / Immunization Division
- **Cost**: 100% Free at all Government Health Facilities, Sub-Centres, Anganwadis, and Village Health Sanitation & Nutrition Days (VHSND).
- **Vaccines at Birth**:
  - BCG (Tuberculosis)
  - OPV 0 dose (Oral Polio)
  - Hepatitis B birth dose (within 24 hours)
- **At 6 Weeks, 10 Weeks, 14 Weeks**:
  - OPV doses 1, 2, 3
  - Pentavalent doses 1, 2, 3 (Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus, Hepatitis B, Hib)
  - Rotavirus vaccine (Severe diarrhoea)
  - Fractional Inactivated Polio Vaccine (fIPV)
  - Pneumococcal Conjugate Vaccine (PCV)
- **At 9-12 Months**:
  - Measles-Rubella (MR) 1st dose
  - JE-1 (Japanese Encephalitis in endemic districts)
  - Vitamin A 1st dose
  - PCV Booster
- **At 16-24 Months**:
  - MR 2nd dose, DPT 1st Booster, OPV Booster, JE-2, Vitamin A dose 2
- **Key Advisory**: Carry the Mother and Child Protection (MCP) card to every session. Mild fever after vaccination is normal; contact ASHA or ANM if high fever persists.
