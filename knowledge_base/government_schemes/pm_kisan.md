# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
## Metadata
- **Category**: Government Schemes
- **Department**: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Eligibility**: All landholding farmer families with cultivable land in their names.
- **Exclusions**: Institutional landholders, farmer families holding constitutional posts, serving or retired government employees, professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs), and taxpayers who paid income tax in last assessment year.
- **Benefits**: Financial benefit of Rs. 6,000 per year per eligible family.
- **Installments**: Payable in three equal installments of Rs. 2,000 each every 4 months (April-July, August-November, December-March).
- **Payment Mode**: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into verified Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts.
- **Mandatory Requirements**:
  1. e-KYC (Aadhaar OTP based on PM-KISAN portal or biometric through CSC centers).
  2. Land seeding (Bhulekh / land records mapped to applicant's name).
  3. Aadhaar-Bank account linking (NPCI mapping).
- **How to Apply**: Online at pmkisan.gov.in (New Farmer Registration) or via Common Service Centre (CSC) or Village Agriculture Officer / Lekhpal.
- **Grievance / Helpline**: Toll-free 155261 / 011-24300606.
