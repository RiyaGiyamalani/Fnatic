# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
## Metadata
- **Category**: Government Schemes
- **Department**: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture
- **Purpose**: Comprehensive crop insurance against non-preventable natural risks (drought, flood, unseasonal rainfall, pests, diseases, hailstorms).
- **Premium Paid by Farmer**:
  - Kharif crops: Maximum 2.0% of Sum Insured.
  - Rabi crops: Maximum 1.5% of Sum Insured.
  - Commercial / Horticultural crops: Maximum 5.0% of Sum Insured.
  - Balance premium is heavily subsidized and shared 50:50 between Central and State Governments.
- **Coverage Stages**:
  1. Prevented Sowing / Planting risk.
  2. Standing crop loss from sowing to harvesting (flood, drought, dry spells, pests).
  3. Post-harvest losses (up to 14 days for crops kept in field for drying).
  4. Localized calamities (hailstorm, landslide, cloudburst).
- **Mandatory Claim Intimation Window**:
  - Farmer must report crop loss within 72 hours of damage through the Crop Insurance App, nearest bank branch, or toll-free helpline 14447.
- **Portal**: pmfby.gov.in
