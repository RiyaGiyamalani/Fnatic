# Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)
## Metadata
- **Category**: Government Schemes
- **Department**: National Health Authority (NHA), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW)
- **Benefit Coverage**: Health insurance cover of up to Rs. 5,00,000 (Five Lakh Rupees) per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.
- **Cashless Treatment**: Fully cashless and paperless access to healthcare services at all empaneled public and private hospitals across India (portable across states).
- **Target Beneficiaries**: Bottom 40% vulnerable rural and urban families identified via Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC 2011) and updated National Food Security Act (NFSA) ration card lists.
- **Family Cap**: No cap on family size or age of family members. Pre-existing medical conditions are covered from day one.
- **Services Included**: Medical examination, consultation, pre-hospitalization (up to 3 days), medicine and medical consumables, diagnostic services, intensive and non-intensive care, post-hospitalization follow-up care (up to 15 days).
- **How to Check Eligibility**:
  1. Visit mera.pmjay.gov.in and enter mobile number / ration card number / SECC name.
  2. Visit nearest Ayushman Mitra at any empaneled government or private hospital.
  3. Contact local ASHA worker or CSC center.
- **Toll-free National Helpline**: 14555.
